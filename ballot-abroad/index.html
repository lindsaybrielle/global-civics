Your ballot

Show me my local races

Mayor, county, school board, state legislature and local measures depend on your exact US address, so we look them up live.

One ZIP code can span several districts, so a street address gives the exact list. Your address is sent only to Google's election data service to look up races, and this app doesn't store it anywhere else. Local candidates get research links, but no written summaries.

Official help

  • FVAP.gov: the federal program for overseas voters. Has the registration form (FPCA), backup ballot (FWAB) and state-by-state rules.
  • Overseas Vote Foundation: nonpartisan guides and a help desk.
  • Ballotpedia sample ballot: candidate bios and survey answers.
  • Vote411 (League of Women Voters): candidates answer the same questions, side by side.

Ballot Abroad is an independent, nonpartisan tool and isn't affiliated with any party, campaign or government agency. Rules and deadlines vary by state, so always confirm with your local election office.
